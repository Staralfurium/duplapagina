Privacy
Policy.

This policy explains how we process personal data submitted through our website, particularly through the quote request form.

1. Data controller

The controller of your data is Dupla Página Lda., based at Rua do Parque Industrial, Lote 36, 4755-539 Várzea, Barcelos, Portugal.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at duplapagina@hotmail.com or +351 253 812 104.

2. Data we collect

When you request a quote, we may collect the information provided in the form: name, company, email, phone number, product specifications, quantities, formats, materials, finishes, deadline, message and links to files you share with us.

Please do not send sensitive personal data or confidential information that is not necessary for us to prepare your quote.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We use this information only to assess your request, clarify details, prepare and send a quote or proposal, and communicate with you about it.

This processing is necessary for steps taken at your request before entering into a contract. If an order follows, the necessary data may also be processed to perform the contract and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Who we share data with

The form is processed by FormSubmit, which sends the message to Dupla Página’s email inbox. Data may also be accessed by technology providers strictly necessary for the operation of our email and website hosting.

We do not sell or disclose your data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Retention period

Quote requests that do not lead to further business are retained for no more than 12 months after the last communication, unless a different period is necessary to meet a legal obligation or defend a legal claim. Data related to an order may be retained for the legally applicable periods.

FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for a limited period; please also consult its privacy information.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection to the processing of your data and, where applicable, data portability. Contact us using the email above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority.

7. Cookies, analytics and third-party services

We use Umami to obtain aggregated website statistics, such as pages visited, referral source, device and browser. This information helps us improve the website and is not used to build advertising profiles.

If you accept the preferences shown on the website, we store your choice locally in your browser together with an indication that you have seen the sustainability notice, so that it is not shown again for 30 days. If you choose to continue without saving, this indication is not retained after the page is closed or refreshed.

The website also loads third-party services such as Google Maps to show our location and Google Fonts for typography. These services may process technical data, such as IP address, according to their own policies.

8. Updates

We may update this policy whenever we change how data is processed or the services used on the website. The version published on this page applies.
